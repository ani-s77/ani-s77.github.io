Coursework from my Georgia Tech M.S. in Analytics.

Autonomous Vehicle Depot Siting In progress

Georgia Tech Data and Visual Analytics · Team of 4 · Aug 2026 – present

Screened 32,007 Cook County parcels to 2,018 candidate depot lots for self-driving taxis in Chicago, by zoning, size and distance to an electrical substation. Designed the evaluation, which tests site choices from 2024 rideshare trips against 2025 trips with a cost sensitivity sweep, and built the k-means baseline the optimization is compared against. Presenting at the course's final poster session.

Tools: Python, SQL, k-means

Passenger Vehicle Supply and Demand Forecasting

Georgia Tech Time Series · Team of 3 · Jan 2026 – May 2026

Showed that supply conditions, travel activity and gas prices did not improve short-term forecasts of U.S. passenger vehicle sales: on a Feb 2022 to Jan 2024 holdout, a simple benchmark (3.3% average error) beat our best model (6.5%). Change-point detection found four breaks in 24 years of monthly sales, and the drivers of sales changed between them.

Tools: Python, statsmodels, ruptures, ARIMAX, SARIMAX, Prophet

Credit Default Prediction In progress

Georgia Tech Computational Data Analytics · Team of 3 · Aug 2026 – present

Predicting which Lending Club loans (2007–2018) will default, training on earlier years and testing on later ones. Built the first model and a baseline comparison of several model types; now checking how rejected applicants differ from approved ones to look for selection bias.

Tools: Python

Dysarthria Speech Understanding via Multi-Task Learning

Georgia Tech Deep Learning · Team of 4 · May 2026 – Aug 2026

Cut Whisper's word error rate on dysarthric speech from 39.2% to 31.3% by building the evaluation and parameter search comparing Whisper and Wav2Vec 2.0. On 28 unseen speakers (UA-Speech), Whisper cut intelligibility error by about 53% against a traditional baseline, and placed severity within one level 94.5% of the time.

Tools: Python, PyTorch, Whisper, Wav2Vec 2.0

Trading Strategy Evaluation

Georgia Tech Machine Learning for Trading · Individual · May 2025 – Aug 2025

Built a rule-based JPMorgan trading strategy from three price indicators that lost less than buy-and-hold on unseen data (-3.4% vs -8.5%). A random forest beat the rules on its training data but fell behind on unseen data, and I reported it as overfitting.

Tools: Python, random forest
