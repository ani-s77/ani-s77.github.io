Credit Default Prediction In progress

Georgia Tech Computational Data Analytics · Team of 3 · Aug 2026 – present

Question: Can we predict which loans will default, and does the model still work on later years it has not seen?

Using Lending Club data (2007–2018), we train on earlier years and test on later ones. We built a first model and compared several basic model types as a baseline, and we are checking how rejected applicants differ from approved ones to look for selection bias.

Tools: Python

Autonomous Vehicle Depot Siting In progress

Georgia Tech Data and Visual Analytics · Team of 4 · Aug 2026 – present

Question: Where should self-driving taxi depots go in Chicago to keep land and empty-driving costs down while meeting rider demand?

We screened 32,007 Cook County parcels down to 2,018 candidate lots by zoning, size and distance to an electrical substation, using Chicago rideshare trips as the demand data. I helped design the evaluation (a cost sensitivity sweep, with sites chosen on 2024 trips and tested on 2025 trips) and built a k-means baseline to check whether the optimization beats simple clustering.

Tools: Python, SQL

Dysarthria Speech Understanding via Multi-Task Learning

Georgia Tech Deep Learning · Team of 4 · May 2026 – Aug 2026

Question: How well do modern speech models handle speech from people with dysarthria?

I built the testing setup and parameter search to compare Whisper and Wav2Vec 2.0. Tuning cut Whisper's word error rate from 39.2% to 31.3%. On 28 unseen speakers (UA-Speech), Whisper cut intelligibility error by about 53% against a traditional baseline. The model flagged impairment even when it missed the exact severity grade: 21.7% exact accuracy, 94.5% within one level.

Tools: Python, PyTorch, Whisper, Wav2Vec 2.0

Passenger Vehicle Supply and Demand Forecasting

Georgia Tech Time Series · Team of 3 · Jan 2026 – May 2026

Question: Do supply conditions, travel activity and gas prices help forecast U.S. vehicle sales better than assuming next month looks like this month?

Change-point detection found four breaks in 24 years of monthly sales (April 2008, August 2011, February 2014, December 2019), and what drives sales changed from period to period. On a Feb 2022 to Jan 2024 holdout, the simple benchmark (3.3% average error) beat our best model (6.5%), so the outside variables explained more but did not improve short-term forecasts.

Tools: Python, statsmodels, ruptures, ARIMAX, SARIMAX, Prophet

Trading Strategy Evaluation

Georgia Tech Machine Learning for Trading · Individual · May 2025 – Aug 2025

Question: Can simple rules built from price indicators beat buy-and-hold on JPMorgan stock, and can a machine learning model do better?

A rule-based strategy built from three indicators lost less than buy-and-hold on unseen data (-3.4% vs -8.5%). A random forest trained on buy, sell and hold labels beat the rules on the data it was built on but fell behind on unseen data, a sign of overfitting, and I reported it that way.

Tools: Python, random forest
