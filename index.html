About

I'm Ani.

My test engineering roles were built on analyzing data. At Snap I supported Ads A/B experiments and traced metric problems to their root cause. At TikTok I built the backend of a load-testing platform data science teams used, sized GPU capacity from GPU, CPU and memory data, and helped set a 60% GPU-utilization standard. At SpaceX's Swarm I automated SQL checks on the satellite messaging backend. At Lightship I built a test platform and its SQL database from scratch, and traced a light-dimming fault to a noisy circuit, informing a planned hardware change.

Over three years of test engineering at Snap, TikTok, SpaceX and Lightship. M.S. Analytics, Georgia Tech (online), graduating May 2027. Available to start immediately, for data analyst and analytics roles in the San Francisco Bay Area or remote.
